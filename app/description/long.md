IISM25 is the official mobile application for the Inter-IIT Sports Meet 2025, hosted by IIT Hyderabad. Designed exclusively for players, coaches, team managers, and event officials, the app provides a centralized platform to access all essential event information and updates throughout the tournament.

Stay informed with the complete event schedule, team details, fixtures, and results across all sports. Follow matches in real time with live scoring, view scorecards, and track overall standings on the leaderboard. Participants can access and manage their profile, check venue-wise schedules, and stay updated with timely notifications from the organizing committee.

The app also includes a dedicated gallery, contact directory of the organizing team, and other important resources to ensure smooth coordination and communication during the event.

A secure login is required for all users, with verification through basic participant details such as name, age, contact information, college email ID, sport, and institute.

IISM25 aims to streamline event operations and enhance the experience for every registered member of the Inter-IIT Sports Meet 2025.
